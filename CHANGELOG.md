# 📝 Changelog

Studio: ORIKAMI.

## 1.3.0 ⭐

- Apple Music style library: pins, categories to show, hide and reorder, "Recently added"
- Recent searches in the Search tab, with a Clear button
- Player: swipe the artwork to change songs, swipe a song to the right to like it
- Player: new row of buttons; add to a playlist and sleep timer in "Up next"
- Songs already played shown in gray above "Up next"
- Home without the ⋮ menu: refresh, shuffle mix and statistics next to the title
- Redesigned listening statistics, with Summary and History tabs
- Demo sample specific to each theme
- Automatic updates, fingerprint and signature verified before installation
- Log export from Settings > Help and troubleshooting, and after a crash
- Fix: the volume bar follows the phone's media volume
- Fix: steadier Calibrated sound, smoother Soft mode

## 1.0 ⭐

- Apple Music style interface: "For you" home, animated full-screen player, synced lyrics
- Playback of the phone's local files
- Interface sized for every screen: phones, foldables, tablets
- Half-open foldable: artwork or lyrics on top, controls and queue below
- Calibrated sound per output (speaker, earphones, car) and Soft mode (crossfade)
- Four themes (Sumi, Washi, Sakura, Matcha) with the matching icon
- Import of Spotify, Deezer and Apple Music playlists
- "All songs" with an alphabetical index
- Android Auto
- Updates offered in the app
